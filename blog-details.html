5 Techniques to Conquer Stage Fright

Sarah Jenkins Oct 12, 2026 · 5 min read

It happens to the best of us. The dry mouth, the sweaty palms, the racing heart. Stage fright is completely normal, but it doesn't have to paralyze you.

Whether you're presenting a project to your class, debating in a national tournament, or giving a toast at a wedding, managing anxiety is the first step to delivering a memorable speech.

1. Shift Your Focus

Anxiety often comes from focusing inward—worrying about how you look or if you'll forget a line. Instead, shift your focus outward to your audience and your message. You are giving them a gift (your knowledge). Focus on delivering that gift.

"The audience wants you to succeed. They are not there to judge you; they are there to listen."

2. The Power of Pause

When we get nervous, we tend to speed up. Intentionally slowing down and using pauses not only makes you look more confident but also gives your brain a second to catch up.

3. Controlled Breathing

Take deep, diaphragmatic breaths before stepping on stage. Inhale for 4 seconds, hold for 4 seconds, and exhale for 6 seconds. This physically lowers your heart rate.
