Course Overview

Learn parliamentary procedure, resolution writing, and diplomatic negotiation for MUN conferences.

Immerse yourself in global diplomacy. Our Model UN training program prepares students to represent different nations, debate complex international issues, and draft comprehensive UN resolutions.
