Competitive Debate Squad

Rigorous tournament preparation for ambitious high school students.

Program Overview

The Competitive Debate Squad is our most intensive program, designed for students who want to compete and win at national and international levels. This program moves beyond the basics of public speaking into complex argumentation, high-speed refutation, and advanced case construction.

What Students Will Learn

  • Advanced logical structures and fallacy identification
  • Flowing techniques for tracking complex arguments
  • High-speed delivery and cross-examination strategies
  • In-depth research methods for evidence-based claims

Curriculum Modules

Learn how to build unbreakable cases. Topics include framing, establishing burdens, and utilizing philosophical frameworks.

Master line-by-line refutation, turning opponents' arguments, and effective cross-examination techniques.

Weekly high-stakes mock debates with detailed judge feedback to prepare for actual competition environments.

Program Info

  • Duration Annual (36 Weeks)
  • Age Group 14-18 Years
  • Class Size Max 8 Students
  • Format Hybrid (Online + Offline)
