About SpeakAcademy

Building the leaders and communicators of tomorrow.

Our Story

Founded in 2015, SpeakAcademy began with a simple belief: every student has a voice, but not every student knows how to use it. What started as a small weekend workshop has grown into a premier institution for communication excellence.

We noticed a gap in traditional education. Schools teach students what to think, but rarely how to articulate those thoughts under pressure. Our founders, former national debate champions, developed a curriculum that bridges this gap, combining rigorous academic debate structures with modern public speaking techniques.

Our Achievements

Over the past decade, our commitment to excellence has resulted in extraordinary success for our students on the global stage.

100% Top University Success

1500+ Global Alumni

Our Mission

To empower students of all ages with the confidence, critical thinking, and communication skills necessary to articulate their ideas clearly and persuasively in any situation.

Our Vision

To be the global standard for communication and debate coaching, recognized for transforming apprehensive speakers into inspiring leaders.
